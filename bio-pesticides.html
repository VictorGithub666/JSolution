Why Choose Bio-Pesticides

Kill the pest. Not your soil.

01

No synthetic residue

Every active agent is a living organism. There are no synthetic molecules, no pre-harvest intervals and no food safety risk.

02

Targets pests, not soil

Biocontrol organisms are highly specific. Beneficial insects, earthworms and soil microbiomes are left completely unharmed.

03

Resistance-proof approach

Living biocontrol agents evolve alongside the pest population, preventing the chemical resistance that plagues synthetic pesticides.
