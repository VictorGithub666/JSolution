Why Choose Bio-Fertilizers

Feed the soil. The soil feeds your crop.

01

Zero chemical pollution

Live microorganisms replace synthetic salts entirely. No runoff, no soil lock-up, no water contamination.

02

Self-sustaining fertility

Beneficial bacteria colonise the root zone and keep working season after season, building long-term soil health.

03

1 Trillion CFU per capsule

Each 1-gram capsule delivers a massive microbial load. A single capsule treats one full acre.
